y8960_opll_voices.bin - preset voices of the Y8960 cartridge's OPLLEX
=====================================================================

y8960_opll_voices.bin holds the voices of the four preset banks the
Y8960 cartridge's OPLLEX picks from (YM2413, YM2423, YMF281 and VRC7).
Without the file, the OPLLEX plays emu2413's presets instead, the
YM2413 ones standing in for the YM2423.

The melodic voices (voices 1-15 of each bank) are from "Copyright free
OPLL(x) ROM patches" by David Viens and Hubert Lamontagne:

  https://github.com/plgDavid/misc/wiki/Copyright-free-OPLL(x)-ROM-patches

  YM2413: April 2015 David Viens, tweaked May 19-21 2015 Hubert Lamontagne
  YM2423: May 4-6 2016 Hubert Lamontagne
  YMF281: May 14 2015 Hubert Lamontagne
  VRC7:   May 15 2015 Hubert Lamontagne & David Viens

The voices were guessed by ear and are not the contents of the chips'
ROMs. They are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike
(CC BY-SA), as that page declares; the page gives no version. The
license covers the voice data only. blueMSX+ itself is distributed
under the GNU General Public License.

The three rhythm entries of each bank are not on that page. They are
the values in ymfm's YM2413 table, and equal NukedOPLL's rhythm
entries.

The file is built from scripts/y8960-opll-voices.txt in the blueMSX+
source by scripts/make-y8960-opll-voices.py.
